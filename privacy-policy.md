Welcome to ScottStadt.com! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our blog, and it outlines your rights and choices regarding your data. By using our site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Data

When you use ScottStadt.com, we may collect personal data in the following ways:

Comments: When you comment on blog posts using your Facebook account, Facebook may share your profile information with us.
Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect demographic and usage data. This includes information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

### 1.2 Non-Personal Data

We may also collect non-personal data that cannot be used to identify you individually, such as aggregated demographic information and browsing patterns.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 To Provide and Improve Our Services

We use the data collected to:

Analyze how visitors use ScottStadt.com and improve user experience.
Serve targeted ads through Google AdSense to better match your interests.

### 2.2 To Communicate with You

If you interact with our site (e.g., commenting on posts), we may use your information to respond to your comments or inquiries.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Data

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

### 3.1 Consent

By using ScottStadt.com, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

### 3.2 Contractual Necessity

Processing personal data may be necessary to fulfill our obligations in connection with the services we provide.

### 3.3 Legitimate Interests

We may process your data based on legitimate interests, such as improving our site and providing targeted advertisements.

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Google Analytics

ScottStadt.com uses Google Analytics to collect and analyze data about site usage. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address and usage patterns. For more details on how Google Analytics handles your data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

### 4.2 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve targeted ads based on your interests. Google AdSense may use cookies to collect information about your visits to our site and other sites you visit. For more information on how Google AdSense uses cookies and your data, please review Google's AdSense Privacy Policy.

### 4.3 Facebook Integration

When you comment on our blog posts via Facebook, Facebook may share your information with us. Please review Facebook's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your information.

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

### 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

### 7.1 Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

### 7.2 Right to Correction

You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data.

### 7.3 Right to Deletion

You may request that we delete your personal data, subject to certain exceptions.

### 7.4 Right to Object

You can object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.

### 7.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to request the transfer of your personal data to another organization or to you in a structured, commonly used format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at scott.stadt@gmail.com.
